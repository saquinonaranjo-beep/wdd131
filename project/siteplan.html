Website Planning Document

Site Name

Silver Elegance Jewelry

This name was chosen to reflect a stylish and modern online store specializing in high-quality silver jewelry. It highlights elegance, beauty, and timeless fashion.

Optional domain: silverelegancejewelry.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to showcase and sell silver jewelry products online. Customers will be able to browse collections, view product details, and learn about the brand. The site will also provide contact information and possibly a shopping cart feature.

Scenarios

  • What types of silver jewelry are available in this store?
  • How can I purchase a necklace or ring from this website?
  • Are the jewelry items made from real silver?

Color Schema

#2c2c2c – Used for headings and text

#c0c0c0 – Used for accents (represents silver)

#f8f8f8 – Background color

Typography

Playfair Display – Used for headings (elegant style)

Open Sans – Used for body text (clean and readable)
