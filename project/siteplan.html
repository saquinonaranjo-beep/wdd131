Website Planning Document

Site Name

FitLife Tracker

This name was chosen because it represents a website focused on tracking fitness progress and maintaining a healthy lifestyle.

Optional domain: fitlifetracker.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to help users track their fitness goals, learn about workouts, and stay motivated. It will provide workout guides, progress tracking tools, and health tips.

Scenarios

  • What exercises should I do as a beginner?
  • How can I track my workout progress effectively?
